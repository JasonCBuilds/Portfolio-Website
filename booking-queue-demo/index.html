Booking & Walk-In Queue

A hybrid scheduling concept for service businesses that need both appointments and same-day walk-ins.

WHY THIS EXISTS

One system for planned and walk-in demand.

Many appointment-based businesses also handle customers who arrive without reservations. This concept puts both flows into one simple experience.

BUSINESS USE CASES

  • Barbershops and salons
  • Auto-detailing and service businesses
  • Repair shops
  • Clinics and appointment-based offices
  • Any business balancing appointments and walk-ins

PRODUCTION EXTENSIONS

  • Real-time availability
  • SMS or email confirmations
  • Customer check-in
  • Provider dashboards
  • Wait-time estimates and notifications
